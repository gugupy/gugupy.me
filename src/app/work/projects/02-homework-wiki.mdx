# Hausaufgaben Wiki

This wiki lets students and teachers create, lookup, list and share homework in a simple, school-oriented interface. Homework creation is AI-assisted: the AI evaluates a draft before you submit, providing feedback on correctness, completeness, clarity, and suggestions for improvement so assignments are higher quality before they are published.

## Key features:
- Create homework
  - Manual creation with fields for description, due date, and subject.
  - AI-assisted review that evaluates the homework is valid.
- Lookup & Search
  - Filters for school, grade and class to narrow results to the exact cohort.
  - Provide Calendar look like UI to lookup homework.
- Share & Collaboration
  - Share assignments via link, export options or directly to a class group.
  - Teachers and students can comment or attach solutions (with configurable visibility).
- Filters
  - School, grade and class act as primary filters;

This combination of AI validation and structured filters (school, grade, class) makes finding and publishing relevant homework fast and reliable.
